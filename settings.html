info About Rentify

Connecting tenants and property owners safely across East Africa.

Rentify is a modern property marketplace designed to make home finding transparent, reliable, and accessible. Whether searching for a studio, apartment, villa, or office space across Kenya's 47 counties down to the ward level, Rentify connects you directly with verified landlords and property managers.

Our mission is to eliminate rental fraud, simplify lease agreements, and provide a seamless digital platform for both tenants and real estate managers.
